# NotebookLM source brief 01: Create the First Opening

## Video purpose

Explain how Lorenzo White would generate qualified interest for Atlas Copco Portable Air and immediately connect that interest to a conversion path. This is the first of three short strategy videos.

This video is presented by **Lorenzo White**, **Founder of ZAH Brand Solutions**, a **Growth Marketing Strategy & Systems Architect**, and a candidate for the **Marketing Communications Manager – APE** role.

Suggested duration: 60–90 seconds.

## The business context

According to Lorenzo's account of the interview, Edson is not simply looking for more marketing activity. The need is for someone who understands how to run campaigns, generate leads, and help convert them.

Today, interest may arrive by phone, website inquiry, trade show, advertising, referral, or direct outreach. Capturing a name in C4C is not enough. If the lead arrives without useful context or a visible next action, it can be deprioritized, passed onward, and eventually become cold.

The first opening must therefore do two jobs:

1. Earn attention from a relevant prospect.
2. Capture enough context to begin a useful sales conversation.

## The strategy

### Lead with a tangible in-person experience

Trade shows, local pop-ups, and targeted industry events are priority touchpoints. The display should use tall application visuals, a real portable air machine, and a knowledgeable representative who can connect the equipment to the visitor's work.

The purpose is not simply to collect booth scans. It is to create a conversation worth continuing.

### Add focused prospecting

**LinkedIn Opportunity Scouting** means identifying relevant local decision-makers—such as fleet, operations, engineering, procurement, and business leaders—and starting a respectful, personal conversation about their work. It is sales prospecting, not recruiting.

Prospecting ads can introduce Portable Air to new audiences on LinkedIn and other approved channels. Referrals, website inquiries, and phone calls can enter the same system.

### Move every interested prospect into the Goal Pulse Check

At an event, a representative can guide the prospect through the **Goal Pulse Check** on a tablet. A QR code lets the prospect continue on their own phone. Digital inquiries receive the same invitation.

The Goal Pulse Check is a focused survey that captures:

- Name, company, role, and site.
- The work or application the prospect needs to support.
- Equipment and capacity needs.
- Buying-team involvement and decision authority.
- Timing and preferred next action.

This makes the lead more useful to sales and shows the prospect that the business is interested in the job they need to accomplish.

## Conversion meaning

The finish line for this stage is not an impression, click, badge scan, or raw lead count. It is a qualified prospect with useful context, permission for relevant follow-up, an assigned next action, and a clear path toward a walkthrough, recommendation, pricing conversation, or quote.

Use this central message:

**Create the first opening. Capture the reason to continue.**

## Recommended story sequence

1. Briefly show the current risk: several inquiries enter C4C; one lacks context and fades after it is passed onward.
2. Introduce Lorenzo and the goal of generating qualified interest that can move toward conversion.
3. Show a premium trade-show or local pop-up experience with a machine and representative.
4. Show LinkedIn Opportunity Scouting and prospecting ads opening direct conversations with relevant local decision-makers.
5. Show the prospect completing the Goal Pulse Check on a tablet or phone.
6. End with a complete lead profile, an assigned sales professional, and a visible next action.

## Narration direction

- Sound like an experienced growth architect explaining a practical system.
- Use concrete marketing terms: qualified interest, prospecting campaign, lead capture, sales conversation, next action, conversion.
- Do not call the prospect a player or describe the process as a mission.
- Do not suggest that a large number of leads is the objective by itself.
- Keep Lorenzo's role and Edson's conversion concern connected throughout.

Useful ideas that may be expressed naturally:

- “A booth scan is not a conversion strategy.”
- “The first interaction should tell sales why the prospect raised their hand.”
- “The Goal Pulse Check turns attention into useful customer context.”
- “Every qualified opening should leave with a next action.”

## Visual direction

Use a polished industrial visual style. Show the progression from attention to context:

**Application display → machine demonstration → conversation → Goal Pulse Check → qualified lead → assigned next action.**

Use icons for event, phone, website, referral, advertising, prospect, survey, sales professional, and next action. Avoid generic stock-business imagery when a machine, worksite, or real customer touchpoint can carry the meaning.

## Required factual boundaries

- This is Lorenzo's proposed strategy, not an approved or live Atlas Copco workflow.
- Describe Edson's need and the handoff issue as Lorenzo's account of the interview.
- Do not claim that Atlas Copco currently lacks trade-show capture, surveys, prospecting, or advertising.
- LinkedIn Opportunity Scouting is professional prospecting, not recruiting or automated mass outreach.
- Any outreach, audience use, or retargeting requires appropriate permission and platform compliance.
- No live leads are being captured or sent to C4C by the prototype.
- Do not guarantee lead volume, conversion rate, revenue, or campaign performance.
