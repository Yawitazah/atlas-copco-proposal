# NotebookLM source brief 03: Keep Ownership After the Handoff

## Video purpose

Address the interview problem directly: prevent a qualified lead from disappearing after a handoff, and show how Atlas Copco can keep ownership, next actions, and commercial outcomes visible. This is the third of three short strategy videos.

This video is presented by **Lorenzo White**, **Founder of ZAH Brand Solutions**, a **Growth Marketing Strategy & Systems Architect**, and a candidate for the **Marketing Communications Manager – APE** role.

Suggested duration: 75–105 seconds.

## The problem Edson described

According to Lorenzo's account of the interview, Edson described a visibility and conversion problem. Leads enter C4C, sales reviews them, and some opportunities—especially those perceived as smaller—may be passed to a dealer, distributor, rental partner, or another team. The handoff can become a point where the original team no longer sees a clearly accountable owner, due next action, or final result.

That does not mean every handoff fails. It means the organization needs a dependable way to see whether the conversation continued.

When nobody can quickly answer who owns the lead, what is due, and what happened, the lead can grow cold while the record appears merely transferred.

Use this line to name the problem:

**The lead was captured. The opportunity was not.**

## The corrected sales path

The main journey must point toward conversion—not toward a dealer handoff as its ending.

Show this path:

**Captured prospect → assigned sales professional → needs confirmed → site walkthrough → personalized equipment recommendation → quote, rental, or purchase decision → recorded outcome.**

The outcome may be won, lost, deferred, referred, or still active, but it must be recorded with an accountable next step while the opportunity remains open.

## How accountability works

### Assign a sales professional

Every qualified lead receives a named internal sales professional who owns the next action. “Assigned” must mean more than a name in a field; it should include a due date and a visible responsibility.

### Preserve the context

The C4C lead record supplies the available contact, account, owner, and activity context. The Goal Pulse Check adds the prospect's application, site, equipment needs, buying-team role, timing, and preferred next step.

That context follows the opportunity so the prospect does not have to restart the conversation after every transfer.

### Treat a partner handoff as a conditional route

A dealer, distributor, rental partner, or another internal team may be the right service route. That route should branch from the main sales path only when appropriate.

If a partner becomes involved:

- The internal sales professional remains visible.
- The receiving partner or team accepts the handoff.
- The next action and due date remain visible.
- The latest recorded interaction can be reviewed.
- The commercial outcome returns to the shared record.

Use this principle:

**A handoff changes who serves the lead. It does not end Atlas Copco's accountability.**

### Keep the loop in review

Sales leaders and the marketing manager should be able to see where each opportunity stopped, who is responsible for the next recorded move, and which sources and follow-up actions result in walkthroughs, quotes, rentals, purchases, or documented losses.

The system supports accountability by making the record visible. It cannot know about calls, visits, or decisions that nobody records.

## Conversion meaning

The purpose of accountability is not surveillance or blame. It is to keep a qualified customer conversation moving and to learn which marketing and sales actions produce a result.

Use this central message:

**Prevent the pass-off. Keep the sales path accountable.**

The final picture should show a prospect reaching a clear commercial result while Atlas Copco retains visibility throughout the journey.

## Recommended story sequence

1. Show leads arriving through phone, website, campaigns, trade shows, and referrals.
2. Show one lower-priority lead entering C4C, being passed onward, losing a clear owner and next action, and turning cold.
3. Display: **“The lead was captured. The opportunity was not.”**
4. Reset the same fictional lead into Lorenzo's accountable pathway.
5. Show the assigned sales professional, confirmed needs, site walkthrough, and personalized recommendation.
6. Show a partner branch only when needed, with accepted handoff and internal owner still attached.
7. Show due next action, recent activity, and customer response remaining visible.
8. End at quote, rental, purchase decision, or another recorded outcome.

## Narration direction

- Be direct about the leak without accusing individual sales representatives or partners.
- Use the words **conversion**, **assigned sales professional**, **accountability**, **accepted handoff**, **due next action**, and **recorded outcome**.
- Explain that C4C is the system of record concept in this proposal while avoiding claims about unverified integration details.
- Keep the story centered on the customer's continuing relationship, not internal monitoring alone.
- End with the conversion result, not with the dealer or distribution handoff.

Useful ideas that may be expressed naturally:

- “A transferred record is not the same as a converted opportunity.”
- “A handoff is not a pass-off.”
- “The internal owner remains visible even when a partner helps serve the customer.”
- “Accountability means knowing who acts next, when it is due, and what outcome followed.”
- “Marketing can improve what it can trace from first interest through recorded result.”

## Visual direction

Begin with a disconnected journey and visibly repair it.

The disconnected version should show incoming lead sources feeding C4C, followed by a fading line, an unowned next action, and a cold lead.

The corrected version should use a strong main line from captured prospect to recorded commercial outcome. A dealer, distributor, rental partner, or other team appears as a side route with a return path to the internal owner and shared record.

Use icons or silhouettes for prospect, C4C record, assigned sales professional, site walkthrough, equipment recommendation, partner, marketing review, quote, rental, purchase, and recorded outcome. Do not make the partner handoff look like the finish line.

## Required factual boundaries

- This is Lorenzo's proposed accountability model, not a description of a verified current Atlas Copco workflow.
- Describe Edson's concern as Lorenzo's account of the interview.
- Do not claim that every smaller opportunity, representative, dealer, distributor, or rental partner produces a lost lead.
- Do not suggest that dealer or distribution involvement is inherently undesirable.
- No live C4C integration, API, export feed, owner assignment, partner acknowledgment, calendar, message, or write-back is active in the prototype.
- Exact C4C edition, fields, APIs, permissions, ownership rules, partner access, and write-back capabilities remain unverified.
- A dashboard reflects only activity recorded by people or connected systems.
- Do not guarantee improved conversion, revenue, or accountability outcomes.
