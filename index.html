    How this connects to SAP C4C +

    Build around C4C.

    SAP Cloud for Customer (C4C) is the existing CRM. Keep it as the system of record for lead ownership, sales activities and commercial outcomes. The experience connects marketing to that record.

    Confirm the edition, administrator, enabled services and integration permissions. Review existing lead and activity workflows with the C4C administrator before configuring the pilot.

    A practical implementation path.

    Validate and configure assignment, required fields, tasks and escalation in C4C first. Use supported APIs or approved middleware to feed the dashboard and write back permitted activities with stable record IDs, access controls and error handling. MCP is optional; its availability has not been established.

    The prototype runs only in this browser. Production would require authenticated users, permissioned C4C writes, audit logs and synchronization. Devices currently explore independently.
